Why local matters

We know how this region actually works

A cottage-country restaurant and a year-round contractor need completely different websites. One lives or dies on a six-week summer window and Google Maps; the other needs to show up when someone searches for an emergency repair in February.

Building here means we already know the seasonal patterns, the towns people actually search for, and which competitors you’re up against on page one.

  • Local keyword targeting by town, not just “Ontario”
