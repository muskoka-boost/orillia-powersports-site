A local studio that treats your business like its own

Muskoka Digital Boost is a web design and development studio based in Orillia, Ontario — built to give local businesses big-agency quality without big-agency prices.

Our story

Why we started

Too many great local businesses across Muskoka and Simcoe County are stuck with outdated websites, overpriced agency retainers, or no website at all. Meanwhile, their customers are searching Google every day — and finding competitors instead.

We started Muskoka Digital Boost to fix that. Our belief is simple: a professional, fast, search-optimized website shouldn’t cost $10,000 or take three months. It should be affordable, quick to launch, and built by someone who actually picks up the phone when you call.

Because we’re local, we understand the seasonal rhythms of cottage country, the trades that keep this region running, and the tourism traffic that peaks every summer. That context shows up in every site we build — from the keywords we target to the way we structure your services pages.

Process

How we work together

Three steps, clear communication, and no surprises along the way.

Discovery Call

A quick, no-pressure conversation to understand your business, audience, and what success looks like for your new website. You’ll get a fixed quote within 24 hours.

Design & Build

We design and build your site with your feedback at every step. You’ll see progress previews throughout — nothing goes live without your sign-off.

Launch & Support

Your site goes live and we handle every technical detail — hosting, backups, security, updates. We stick around for the long haul.
