About us

Turn unwanted items into cash

Bring in your unwanted metal and get paid on the spot. We buy ferrous and non-ferrous metals including steel, copper, aluminium, brass, stainless, and more.

Whether you're a homeowner cleaning out a garage, a contractor with jobsite scrap, or a business needing regular pickups, our yard offers honest weights, competitive prices, and same-day payment. We also provide roll-off bins, flatbed hauling, and on-site collection for larger loads.

Turn your scrap into cash while helping the environment — fast, friendly service every time.

  • Providing recycling services since 2000
  • Honest weights taken on our own scale
  • Same-day payment, every time
  • Staff who will actually answer your scrap questions
